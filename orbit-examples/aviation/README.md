# Aviation & Aerospace Operations

This directory contains Orbit-RS examples for the **Aviation Industry**, covering commercial airlines, private aviation, and emerging urban air mobility.

## Scenarios

### [Commercial Airline Ops](commercial_airline_ops/)
- **MRO Systems**: Maintenance, Repair, and Overhaul tracking.
- **Flight Data Analysis**: QAR/FDR log processing.

### [Private Aviation](private_aviation/)
- **Fractional Ownership**: Contract and share management (NetJets style).
- **Concierge Services**: VIP profile and request handling.

### [Urban Air Mobility (eVTOL)](urban_air_mobility/)
- **Telemetry**: High-frequency battery and motor safety data.
- **Vertiport Ops**: Real-time landing pad and charger slot management.
