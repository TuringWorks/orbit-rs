# Urban Air Mobility (eVTOL)

Examples for **Electric Vertical Take-off and Landing (eVTOL)** vehicle operations and Vertiport management.

## Scenarios

### 1. Battery & Motor Telemetry (CQL)
**File**: [`cql/01_telemetry.cql`](cql/01_telemetry.cql)
- High-frequency logging of RPM, vibration, and battery cell temperatures for safety and predictive maintenance.

### 2. Vertiport Slot Management (Redis)
**File**: [`redis/02_vertiport_ops.redis`](redis/02_vertiport_ops.redis)
- Real-time management of landing pads, charging stations, and airspace density.
