# Government Services Examples - OrbitRS

## Overview

Citizen services, permits, tax processing, emergency response, and infrastructure management with ML fraud detection.

## Architecture

- **PostgreSQL**: Citizens, permits, licenses, tax records, infrastructure
- **MongoDB**: Application forms, documents, public records
- **Redis**: Real-time service requests, emergency dispatch
- **ML Models**: Fraud detection, service optimization, resource allocation

## Features

- Citizen identity and services
- Permit and licensing workflows
- Tax collection and processing
- Public records management
- Emergency response coordination
- Infrastructure asset management
- Fraud detection and prevention

## Compliance

- GDPR compliance
- Data privacy and protection
- Audit trails
- Public records access
